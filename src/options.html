Zweitschlüssel

Unofficial OTP autofill for TU Graz. Not made, endorsed or reviewed by TU Graz. Your key never leaves this browser, and the extension can't make network requests.

1. Get a setup key from TU Graz

  1. Open otp.tugraz.at and add a new TOTP token (you need the one-time registration code, or your current OTP if you already have a token).
  2. Copy the otpauth:// link shown with the QR code, or the key itself.
  3. Leave that page open. You will type a code from here into “Verify token” there.

This uses 1 of your 4 TU Graz tokens. Keep a second token on your phone in case this browser profile is lost.

2. Paste it here

A pasted link fills these in. For a bare key, TU Graz's documented values (SHA256, 6 digits, 60 s) are used.

3. Protect it

You type it once per browser session. The key is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 600,000 rounds).

Without a passphrase, anyone who can read your Chrome profile can make your TU Graz codes forever. Only do this on a computer that only you use, with disk encryption.
